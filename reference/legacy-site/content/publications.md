Our first pub! (2024)

We provide a first glance at the GMToL data and present a systematic review of gut microbiomes across the tree of life. We also provide an overview of best practices for comparative gut microbiome research and the theoretical considerations researchers should undertake when comparing gut microbiomes across the animal kingdom.
